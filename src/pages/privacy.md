naandalist.com is the personal website of Listiananda Apriliawan. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, how it is used, and which third-party services are involved. By using the site you agree to this policy and the [Terms of Use](/terms).

## What is collected

### Analytics

The site uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views and measure loading performance. They work without cookies and do not identify individual visitors. Collected data includes the page visited, the referring site, the browser, the operating system, the device type, and the country.

### Server logs

The site is hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Like any host, Vercel processes request information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

### The chat assistant

If you use the chat page, your messages are sent to Google Gemini, or OpenAI when Gemini is unavailable, to write a reply. Your latest message is also turned into a search query against a [Pinecone](https://www.pinecone.io/legal/privacy/) index of my CV and notes, so the assistant can answer from them. The site itself does not store your conversations. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat. See the privacy policies of [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Cookies and browser storage

- **`rl_token` cookie.** The chat sets one signed cookie that counts your messages for the day, to keep usage within a daily limit. It holds only the date and a count, and it expires after a day.
- **Local storage.** Your theme and sound preferences are saved in your browser so the site remembers them. They never leave your device.
- **Session storage.** A flag that plays the page entrance animation only once per visit.

You can clear or block these in your browser settings. The site keeps working without them.

## Content from other services

Some parts of the page load directly from other services, which then receive your IP address and browser details:

- **Last.fm**, through a public proxy, for the song I listened to most recently, with cover art from Last.fm or YouTube.
- **Hackatime** for my coding statistics.
- **GitHub** for avatars and project previews.
- **Cloudinary** for images inside blog posts.

Links to other websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

## What is not collected

This is a personal portfolio and blog. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no payments. It does not sell or share your data for advertising.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their information.
